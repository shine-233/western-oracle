# 挪威卢恩诗 + 冰岛卢恩诗 (挪威诗约13世纪 / 冰岛诗约15世纪)
# 英译: Bruce Dickins, Runic and Heroic Poems of the Old Teutonic Peoples, 1915 (公版)
# 来源: Wikisource https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Rune_poems
# 抓取日期: 2026-08-25
# 说明: 两诗均为年轻弗萨克(16符文)体系。清洗阶段按通行对应关系映射到老弗萨克24符文，
#       其中年轻弗萨克 Ýr 映射到老弗萨克 Algiz（字形演变关系）。
#       老弗萨克中的 Gebo/Wunjo/Eihwaz/Perthro/Ehwaz/Ingwaz/Othala/Dagaz 在年轻弗萨克中被弃用，
#       故无挪威/冰岛诗节，属预期缺口而非漏采。

== THE NORWEGIAN RUNE POEM ==

[FÉ]
NO: Fé vældr frænda róge; føðesk ulfr í skóge.
EN: Fe — Wealth is a source of discord among kinsmen; the wolf lives in the forest.

[ÚR]
NO: Úr er af illu jarne; opt løypr ræinn á hjarne.
EN: Ur — Dross comes from bad iron; the reindeer often races over the frozen snow.

[ÞURS]
NO: Þurs vældr kvinna kvillu; kátr værðr fár af illu.
EN: Thurs — Giant causes anguish to women; misfortune makes few men cheerful.

[ÓSS]
NO: Óss er flæstra færða för; en skalpr er sværða.
EN: As — Estuary is the way of most journeys; but a scabbard is of swords.

[REIÐ]
NO: Ræið kveða rossom væsta; Reginn sló sværðet bæzta.
EN: Reidh — Riding is said to be the worst thing for horses; Reginn forged the finest sword.

[KAUN]
NO: Kaun er barna bǫlvan; bǫl gørver nán fǫlvan.
EN: Kaun — Ulcer is fatal to children; death makes a corpse pale.

[HAGALL]
NO: Hagall er kaldastr korna; Kristr skóp hæimenn forna.
EN: Hagall — Hail is the coldest of grain; Christ created the world of old.

[NAUDHR]
NO: Nauðr gerer næppa koste; nøktan kælr í froste.
EN: Naudhr — Constraint gives scant choice; a naked man is chilled by the frost.

[ÍS]
NO: Ís kǫllum brú bræiða; blindan þarf at læiða.
EN: Isa — Ice we call the broad bridge; the blind man must be led.

[ÁR]
NO: Ár er gumna góðe; get ek at ǫrr var Fróðe.
EN: Ar — Plenty is a boon to men; I say that Frodi was generous.

[SÓL]
NO: Sól er landa ljóme; lúti ek helgum dóme.
EN: Sol — Sun is the light of the world; I bow to the divine decree.

[TÝR]
NO: Týr er æinendr ása; opt værðr smiðr blása.
EN: Tyr — Tyr is a one-handed god; often has the smith to blow.

[BJARKAN]
NO: Bjarkan er laufgrønstr líma; Loki bar flærða tíma.
EN: Bjarkan — Birch has the greenest leaves of any shrub; Loki was fortunate in his deceit.

[MAÐR]
NO: Maðr er moldar auki; mikil er græip á hauki.
EN: Madhr — Man is an augmentation of the dust; great is the claw of the hawk.

[LǪGR]
NO: Lǫgr er, fællr ór fjalle foss; en gull ero nosser.
EN: Logr — A waterfall is a river which falls from a mountain-side; but ornaments are of gold.

[ÝR]
NO: Ýr er vetrgrønstr viða; vænt er, er brennr, at sviða.
EN: Yr — Yew is the greenest of trees in winter; it is wont to crackle when it burns.

== THE ICELANDIC RUNE POEM ==

[FÉ]
IS: Fé er frænda róg ok flæðar viti ok grafseiðs gata, aurum fylkir.
EN: Fé — Wealth: source of discord among kinsmen, and fire of the sea, and path of the serpent.

[ÚR]
IS: Úr er skýja grátr ok skára þverrir ok hirðis hatr, umbre vísi.
EN: Ur — Shower: lamentation of the clouds, and ruin of the hay-harvest, and abomination of the shepherd.

[ÞURS]
IS: Þurs er kvenna kvöl ok kletta búi ok varðrúnar verr, Saturnus þengill.
EN: Thurs — Giant: torture of women, and cliff-dweller, and husband of a giantess.

[ÓSS]
IS: Óss er algingautr ok ásgarðs jöfurr, ok valhallar vísi, Jupiter oddviti.
EN: Oss — God: aged Gautr, and prince of Ásgardr, and lord of Vallhalla.

[REIÐ]
IS: Reið er sitjandi sæla ok snúðig ferð ok jórs erfiði, iter ræsir.
EN: Reid — Riding: joy of the horsemen, and speedy journey, and toil of the steed.

[KAUN]
IS: Kaun er barna böl ok bardaga för ok holdfúa hús, flagella konungr.
EN: Kaun — Ulcer: disease fatal to children, and painful spot, and abode of mortification.

[HAGALL]
IS: Hagall er kaldakorn ok krapadrífa ok snáka sótt, grando hildingr.
EN: Hagall — Hail: cold grain, and shower of sleet, and sickness of serpents.

[NAUD]
IS: Nauð er Þýjar þrá ok þungr kostr ok vássamlig verk, opera niflungr.
EN: Naud — Constraint: grief of the bond-maid, and state of oppression, and toilsome work.

[ÍSS]
IS: Íss er árbörkr ok unnar þak ok feigra manna fár, glacies jöfurr.
EN: Iss — Ice: bark of rivers, and roof of the wave, and destruction of the doomed.

[ÁR]
IS: Ár er gumna góði ok gott sumar, algróinn akr, annus allvaldr.
EN: Ar — Plenty: boon to men, and good summer, and thriving crops.

[SÓL]
IS: Sól er skýja skjöldr ok skínandi röðull ok ísa aldrtregi, rota siklingr.
EN: Sol — Sun: shield of the clouds, and shining ray, and destroyer of ice.

[TÝR]
IS: Týr er einhendr áss ok ulfs leifar ok hofa hilmir, Mars tiggi.
EN: Tyr — Tyr: god with one hand, and leavings of the wolf, and prince of temples.

[BJARKAN]
IS: Bjarkan er laufgat lim ok lítit tré ok ungsamligr viðr, abies buðlungr.
EN: Bjarken — Birch: leafy twig, and little tree, and fresh young shrub.

[MAÐR]
IS: Maðr er manns gaman ok moldar auki ok skipa skreytir, homo mildingr.
EN: Madr — Man: delight of man, and augmentation of the earth, and adorner of ships.

[LÖGR]
IS: Lögr er vellanda vatn ok viðr ketill ok glömmungr grund, lacus lofðungr.
EN: Lögr — Water: eddying stream, and broad geysir, and land of the fish.

[ÝR]
IS: Ýr er bendr bogi ok brotgjarnt járn ok fífu fárbauti, arcus ynglingr.
EN: Yr — Yew: bent bow, and brittle iron, and giant of the arrow.
